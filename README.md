## 🌐 Overview

**ATLAS AI** is an intelligent clinical trial data platform that transforms fragmented
clinical datasets into a unified **Patient 360** view.

Instead of manually navigating multiple clinical data domains, investigators can
interact with the study using **natural language**.

💬 Ask a question.  
🔎 ATLAS AI retrieves the relevant records.  
🧠 Clinical rules are applied deterministically.  
🛡️ Evidence is validated.  
📋 The answer is returned with traceable citations.

### Example

> **"Which patients may meet Hy's law criteria?"**

ATLAS AI doesn't simply generate an answer.

It:

```text
Understand the question
        ↓
Identify relevant clinical domains
        ↓
Retrieve patient records
        ↓
Normalize clinical values
        ↓
Apply protocol-aware rules
        ↓
Validate supporting evidence
        ↓
Generate an auditable answer
